About Melody

"I'm drawn to the search for beautiful things. Not collecting for the sake of it — but the quiet pleasure of finding something that feels exactly right, and understanding why.

That instinct led here: to The Three Edit, where finding becomes curating, and curating becomes a way of helping you feel something in a space. Not just see it.

I believe a home isn't decorated — it's assembled, slowly, from things that earn their place."
